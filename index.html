features
Small tool.
Does one thing right.
Instant, every time
Fires the moment a submission lands. Not in batches, not on a schedule. The form submits and your Discord channel knows.
Custom message templates
Write your own message using {{Field Name}} variables. Include what matters, leave out what doesn't.
Works with any channel
Paste a webhook URL, pick your channel. That's it. No bot permissions, no OAuth, no server invites required.
2-minute setup
Install from the Workspace Marketplace. Open a form. Paste webhook. Save. You're done. Seriously, that's the whole process.
Your data stays yours
Runs entirely inside Google Apps Script. Form responses go directly from Google to Discord — they never touch our servers.
Formatted embeds
Every response arrives as a clean Discord embed with labeled fields. Readable at a glance, not a wall of JSON.
