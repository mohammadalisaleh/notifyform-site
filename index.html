How it works
Up and running in 3 steps
Works with any Google Form. No coding required.
1
Install the add-on
Install from the Google Workspace Marketplace with one click. Open your Google Form and launch it from the Extensions menu.
2
Paste your webhook URL
Get a webhook URL from Discord, Slack, or Telegram and paste it into the add-on. Hit save and test in one click.
3
Toggle on and done
Turn notifications on. Every new form submission fires an instant notification to your channel with all the form fields.
