Getting Started
How to install and set up NotifyForm
1

Install from the Google Workspace Marketplace

Go to the Google Workspace Marketplace™ and search for "NotifyForm". Click Install and grant the requested permissions. The add-on will be available in all your Google Forms™.

2

Open a Google Form and launch NotifyForm

Open any Google Form™ you want to add notifications to. Click Extensions in the top menu, then click Open NotifyForm. The sidebar will open on the right side.

3

Get your Discord webhook URL

In Discord, open the channel you want notifications in → click the gear icon (Edit Channel) → Integrations → Webhooks → New Webhook → Copy Webhook URL. Need more help? Discord's webhook guide ↗

4

Paste the webhook URL and toggle on

In the NotifyForm sidebar, click + Add, paste your Discord webhook URL, and hit Save. Then toggle Active on this form to on. You're done — every new submission will fire a Discord notification instantly.
